About Me

Pranav Srikrishnan

Software Developer & ML Engineer

I'm a Computer Engineering student at K.J. Somaiya School of Engineering (KJSSE), Mumbai, with a CGPA of 8.4. I'm simultaneously pursuing a BS in Data Science from IIT Madras, where I've cleared the foundational level — a programme I chose to deepen my theoretical grounding in statistics and computation.

My interests sit at the crossroads of machine learning, software engineering, and applied mathematics. I enjoy building things end-to-end — from designing normalized database schemas to training neural networks for real-world problems like OCR and sentiment analysis.

Outside of tech, I'm an accomplished Mridangam player (winner of ICMDA and Akhil Bharatiya Sangh), a recognized Pianist at Poona Sangeetha Sabha, a national-level Table Tennis player, and a zonal-level basketball player. I lead the Illuminati Literary Club at KJSCE and am part of the Maya Somaiya Musical Team.

I speak English, Hindi, Marathi, and Tamil — and I'm always looking for the next challenge, whether in a codebase or on a stage.
